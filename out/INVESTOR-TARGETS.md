## The strategic fact this list is built on

**CASAFARI raised $135M doing property data for Spain and Portugal.** Same
country, same asset class, same buyer. They are the proof that this category
gets funded at scale in exactly your market — and their cap table is a list of
people who have already decided this thesis is real and don't need convincing
that Spanish property data is a business.

That is the single most valuable thing on this page. You are not cold-pitching
an idea. You are approaching investors who already wrote cheques for the
category and asking them to look at the part CASAFARI does *not* have.

**Your wedge against them, stated honestly:** CASAFARI aggregates listings
broadly across four countries. Avena records what listings *did* — the price
path, the delisting, the absorption — in a segment where that history is
destroyed daily and no registry keeps it. Five days deep today. That is a small
number and you must say it out loud; the argument is the derivative, not the
level. Nobody can start this and be five days behind you. They start at zero,
tonight.

## Honest stage assessment

At €0 revenue and 21 clicks/month, **Tier 3 and Tier 4 plus FJ Labs are the
realistic conversations.** Tier 1's Series A leads (Prudence, Lakestar) came in
at CASAFARI's scale, not at yours — approach them in round two, or for an intro
rather than a cheque.

Do not paper over the traction. Every one of these funds will find it in ten
minutes, and being the founder who stated it first is worth more than the
number itself. What you have instead of traction:

- A licensed Spanish estate agency — you hold the transaction side, which almost no proptech founder does
- 1,996 units scored daily across 97 towns, 31 fields at 100% coverage
- An AVM with a **measured** error rate (15.8% median, in-sample, published with caveats)
- A daily observation ledger nobody else on earth is keeping
- Published, versioned, CC-BY open data across four independent surfaces

## Order of operations

1. **FJ Labs first.** Highest cold-inbound rate in venture, fastest decision, smallest downside. If they pass, it costs you a week.
2. **Pi Labs and Concrete VC** — best stage fit, and Concrete's filter is literally "real-estate credentials plus technology."
3. **Skyfall and Antler** — home market, explicitly pre-PMF.
4. Tier 1 and 2 once one of the above is in, or for warm intros.

Send the capability PDF (`out/Avena-Data-Capability.pdf`) as the attachment. It
is generated from the live database, states its own limits, and is the single
most credible artefact you own.
